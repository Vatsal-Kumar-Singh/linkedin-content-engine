# Hooks — the first two lines

**65% of readers never click "see more".** For most of your audience the first
two or three lines are not an introduction to the post, they are the post. Write
them as though nothing follows.

## The two jobs

A hook has to do both at once, and most drafts only do the first:

1. **Stop the scroll** — say something that interrupts.
2. **Signal who it is for** — so the right reader knows to stay and the wrong one
   leaves without costing you a shallow impression.

Job two is the one people miss. A hook that stops everyone equally is a hook that
brings the algorithm an audience with no interest in you, which damages the topic
fingerprint. Naming the reader is not a narrowing cost, it is a targeting gain.

## Length

- First line **under ten words**.
- Do not run a sentence across the truncation point. Whatever sits either side of
  "see more" must each make sense alone.

## What has stopped working

The explicit hook formulas are saturated. In 2026 these read as a tell that the
post was written to a template:

> "Unpopular opinion:" · "Here's what nobody tells you about…" · "I'll say it:" ·
> "Let that sink in." · "Read that again."

The lever underneath each of them still works — contradiction, secret, stakes.
What no longer works is announcing that you are pulling it. Use the lever, drop
the announcement.

**Weak:** *Unpopular opinion: your pipeline is not the problem.*
**Better:** *Your pipeline is not the problem. Nothing was watching it.*

## Openings that fit this account

Grounded in the pains and in what the niche is not already doing:

| Shape | Example |
|---|---|
| The scene | *Ask when the pipeline last ran clean. Watch the room.* |
| The open question | *How long would a broken pipeline go unnoticed here?* |
| The flat statement | *Your analysts checked those jobs again this morning.* |
| The correction | *That is not a bad run of quarters. It is how the contract was written.* |

## Questions

An open question is often stronger than a statement, because a claim can be
rejected and a question can only be answered. But **only open ones** — a headline
opening on a yes/no auxiliary hands the reader an exit:

> ✗ *Is your data quality good enough?* — answered "no", scrolled past
> ✓ *How long would a broken pipeline go unnoticed here?* — cannot be answered without checking

This is enforced as gate C0.4. Do not make every post a question: a feed of
question marks reads as a quiz format. Roughly half is a sensible ceiling.
Full reasoning in `kb/ad-craft.md` §4b.
